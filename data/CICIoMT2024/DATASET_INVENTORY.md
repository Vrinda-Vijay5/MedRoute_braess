## Selection and leakage controls

Only files whose names begin `Benign_` were selected. Other files are rejected with a recorded reason and are not treated as ordinary hospital congestion. The training split alone derives workload parameters. The test split is held out for distribution comparison.

Selected fields: `Protocol Type`, `Duration`, `Rate`, `Srate`, `Drate`, `TCP`, `UDP`, `AVG`, `Tot size`, `IAT`, and `Number`. No label column is present in the discovered benign files.

## Limitations

- Rows are CICIoMT aggregate feature records, not patient measurements or packet replay traces.
- No clinical priority labels are present; existing MedRoute class labels remain an explicit synthetic policy.
- The publisher does not specify Rate units on the dataset page; Rate*AVG is used only as a relative score whose quantile ratios are scaled to a synthetic 500 kbit/s median engineering load.
- IAT units are not assumed and IAT is not used to configure packet timing.

Official scope/source: [UNB Canadian Institute for Cybersecurity CICIoMT2024](https://www.unb.ca/cic/datasets/iomt-dataset-2024.html). The publisher describes an IoMT security dataset with extracted CSV features grouped into train/test splits.

The processed samples support reproducible characterization, not claims about real patient physiology, Braess labels, link latency, utilization, or SDN forwarding.
