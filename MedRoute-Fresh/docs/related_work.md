# Preliminary related-work and prior-art notes

This is a verified starting bibliography, not an exhaustive systematic review. Bibliographic metadata and abstracts were checked against publisher, institutional, or specification pages on 2026-10-29. The current search did not establish a novel SDN-specific Braess contribution; novelty remains unproven.

## Supported claim and remaining review

The evidence presently supports only this narrow claim: MedRoute contains a deterministic measured-cost guard and a separate verified Wardrop example, with historical Mininet evidence under one saved setup. It does not yet support a claim that CBDR is novel relative to SDN traffic engineering, QoS routing, or topology-design work.

Before submission, search IEEE Xplore, ACM DL, SpringerLink, ScienceDirect, and Google Scholar systematically for SDN traffic engineering under Braess-like effects, topology additions/removals, centrally controlled versus selfish routing, and healthcare QoS. Record inclusion criteria, full texts, datasets, assumptions, metrics, limitations, and direct differences. Independently verify all bibliographic details and compare with work published after the sources listed above.
