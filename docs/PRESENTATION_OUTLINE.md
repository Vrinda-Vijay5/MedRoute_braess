# MedRoute Presentation Outline

1. **Motivation** — why added links can harm equilibrium performance.
2. **Objectives and scope** — deterministic SDN; no AI/ML in this phase.
3. **Architecture** — dataset/catalog, policy, guard, controller, OVS, evidence.
4. **Canonical proof** — 65 to 80 analytical Braess cost increase.
5. **Routing guard** — paired telemetry, threshold, fail-safe behavior.
6. **OpenFlow enforcement** — baseline, shortcut, prevention, recovery rules.
7. **Criticality policy** — ECG, ICU, WEARABLE, BACKGROUND queues.
8. **Congestion results** — receiver throughput/loss and queue evidence.
9. **Resilience** — failed S1-S2 link and recovered path.
10. **Evidence package** — raw JSON, JSONL, flow dumps, SQLite, plots.
11. **Limitations** — synthetic traffic, small topology, no clinical inference.
12. **Reproduction** — WSL workflow and dashboard.
